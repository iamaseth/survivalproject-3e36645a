# BoBo — Survival Tabs Influencer TikTok Search

## Purpose
Find enough new TikTok creator candidates to support a target of 50 usable creator contacts per day.

## BoBo's daily job
Do 5–10 TikTok searches per day. Keep the process simple:

1. Click the TikTok search link provided by ChatGPT.
2. Review/open the TikTok search results page.
3. Click the Obsidian Web Clipper and save the whole search-results page.
4. Return to ChatGPT and type `$done`.
5. ChatGPT marks that search complete and gives the next clickable search.

BoBo does NOT need to copy individual creator profiles, find emails, score creators, message creators, promise samples, or discuss payment.

## Daily target
- 5–10 search terms
- Enough candidates for 50 new usable creator contacts after Seth/ChatGPT filters them.

## Tracking rule
ChatGPT should track which search terms BoBo reports completed during the session and give her the next term one at a time. Rotate terms across days to reduce duplicate creator results.

## Handoff to Seth
There is ONE master Obsidian vault: Seth's.

BoBo creates TikTok search-result clippings on her computer. Seth later copies BoBo's new .md clipping files into the Clippings folder of his master Obsidian vault and manually brings the new files into ChatGPT.

ChatGPT then:
1. Extracts creator accounts from all 5–10 search-result files.
2. Combines results.
3. Removes duplicate creators.
4. Filters poor-fit accounts.
5. Counts usable creators toward the 50/day target.
6. Builds the creator/contact list.
7. Records which search terms produced the strongest results.

## First test result
The initial `emergency preparedness` clipping returned 20 TikTok video results representing 19 unique creator accounts before qualification. Use this as evidence that full-page clipping is faster than manually copying every profile.
