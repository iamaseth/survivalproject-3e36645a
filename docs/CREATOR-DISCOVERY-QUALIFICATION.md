# Survival Tabs — Creator Discovery Qualification

## Primary campaign target

The primary influencer campaign is built for **micro- and nano-creators with 20,000 followers/subscribers or less**.

Do not prioritize large influencers for the main campaign.

There is **no minimum follower requirement**. A creator with only a few hundred followers can qualify if the audience is relevant, the creator is active, and the content is useful for Survival Tabs.

### Preferred size bands

| Followers / subscribers | Priority |
|---|---|
| Under 1,000 | Include promising active niche creators |
| 1,000–5,000 | Very high priority |
| 5,001–10,000 | Very high priority |
| 10,001–20,000 | High priority |
| Over 20,000 | Exclude from the primary campaign |

The objective is **large-scale discovery of relevant small creators**, not celebrity-influencer outreach.

## Why small creators fit Survival Tabs

Prioritize creators who could realistically be interested in one or more of the following:

- Receiving free Survival Tabs to test
- Making an honest product review
- Including Survival Tabs in a gear review
- Testing Survival Tabs in an emergency kit
- Adding Survival Tabs to a bug-out bag or go bag
- Comparing Survival Tabs with other emergency foods
- Creating a taste test
- Including Survival Tabs in camping or backpacking content
- Participating in an affiliate program

Audience relevance matters more than raw size. A creator with 700 highly engaged survival followers can be more valuable to this campaign than an unrelated creator with 100,000 followers.

## Relevance qualification

A creator should ideally have **20,000 followers/subscribers or less** and at least one strong Survival Tabs connection.

High-value topic connections include:

- Preparedness
- Prepping
- Survival
- Emergency food
- Survival food reviews
- Emergency gear reviews
- Bug-out bags
- Go bags
- 72-hour kits
- Everyday carry / EDC
- Camping
- Hiking
- Backpacking
- Bushcraft
- Homesteading
- Off-grid living
- Food storage
- Overlanding
- RV / van life
- Vehicle emergency preparedness
- Disaster preparedness
- Hurricane / storm preparedness
- Outdoor gear
- Amazon gear reviews
- Emergency communications / ham radio
- Family preparedness

Related niche creators can qualify when there is a clear, reasonable Survival Tabs use case even if they do not use the word “prepper.”

## Activity

Prefer creators who have posted within approximately the **last 90 days**.

Do not automatically reject a creator who posts less frequently when the channel is clearly still active and strongly relevant. Activity is a ranking signal, not an inflexible cutoff.

## Contactability

Strongly prioritize creators with a legitimate public contact path:

- Public business email
- Website or contact page
- Instagram
- TikTok
- YouTube public contact information
- Other public creator/business social account

**Never guess email addresses.**

A creator can remain a candidate without an email if a legitimate public contact route exists, but public business email is the preferred outreach path.

## Quality standard

The goal is not to fill the CRM with weak records merely to reach a number.

Primary qualification standard:

**Relevant + Active + ≤20K + Contactable + Appropriate for Survival Tabs**

When information is incomplete, keep the creator in the candidate/enrichment stage rather than pretending the record is fully qualified.

## Volume targets

### Final target

Find at least **1,000 qualified creators with 20,000 followers/subscribers or less**.

### Discovery target

Ideally discover **2,000–3,000 candidates first**, then filter and enrich them down to the strongest 1,000.

This larger candidate pool is intentional. We expect to remove duplicates, inactive channels, irrelevant organizations, weak fits, and creators with no legitimate contact route.

## Ranking order

When choosing which candidates to research and enrich first, use this order:

1. Strong Survival Tabs relevance
2. 1,000–10,000 followers/subscribers
3. Legitimate public business email or contact route
4. Posted within roughly the last 90 days
5. Under 1,000 followers but highly relevant and active
6. 10,001–20,000 followers with strong relevance

Do not promote an unrelated creator merely because the creator has more followers.

## Exclusions from the primary campaign

Normally exclude or deprioritize:

- Creators over 20,000 followers/subscribers
- Government agencies
- Institutions rather than individual/creator-led channels
- Irrelevant businesses
- Clearly inactive/dead channels
- Channels with no meaningful Survival Tabs connection
- Spam, scraped, fake, or unverifiable creator records
- Guessed or fabricated contact information

## Data safety

Discovery and enrichment are **add-only** operations.

- Never bulk-delete the existing creator database.
- Never replace reviewed creator history.
- Deduplicate new candidates before promotion.
- Enrich an existing record rather than creating a duplicate where possible.
- Preserve Do Not Contact suppression.
- Discovery/import must never automatically send outreach email.
