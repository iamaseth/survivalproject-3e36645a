# Survival Tabs Influencer Screening Standard v1.0 — LOCKED

Effective 2026-10-13. Apply uniformly to all 5,439 candidates and future batches. User-approved screening criteria supersede older mandatory US proof and broad cooking/product-review eligibility. Do not change thresholds between batches without explicit approval and a version bump.

## Objective
Reduce Seth's manual workload: only strong, evidence-backed creator candidates appear in the active second-review queue. Preserve all rejected/uncertain records in a searchable backlog, not active review. No automatic outreach or manual decision overrides.

## Evidence
Read entire captured profile, bio, links, captions, descriptions and hashtags. Bio alone can establish a clear fit; missing captions, email or US proof is not rejection evidence. Never claim to have watched uncaptured video. Distinguish source facts from inference. Check overall account pattern, not isolated keywords. Original BoBo 500 search terms are discovery context, not automatic qualification.

## Fit
CORE: civilian preparedness, emergency food/water storage, camping, hiking, backpacking, backcountry outdoor adventure, wilderness skills, off-grid self-reliance, homesteading, food preservation, family emergency readiness.
CONDITIONAL: outdoor fishing expeditions, remote RV/van camping, remote adventure travel, trail meals/nutrition, endurance adventures, reviews of camping/outdoor/emergency gear, only when a recurring account theme or explicit bio establishes the use case.
NOT FIT ALONE: general cooking, generic nutrition/fitness coaching, general product reviews, Amazon finds, everyday home gadgets, ordinary vacations/city tourism, fashion, beauty, generic lifestyle, generic DIY. A credible niche crossover can override this exclusion when documented.
Commercial brands and competing retailers are not independent creator outreach candidates. Non-US location is disqualifying only with credible evidence of actual non-US base; missing location is neutral.

## Decisions
A PASS INITIAL SCREENING: specific bio or recurring caption evidence of core/conditional fit; account suitable for creator outreach. Prepare one unique grounded complimentary-sample DM, no obligation to post, signed — Rena, Survival Tabs. Do not send. Only A goes to Seth's active second-review queue.
INCOMPLETE CAPTURE (technical exception, not a qualification decision): only demonstrably truncated, wrong-account, or extraction-failed Markdown. Record defect and send to BoBo for recapture. No missing email, captions, or US location alone constitutes a capture defect.
C REJECT: available intact Markdown does not establish relevant niche, documented unrelated niche, unsuitable brand account, or credible non-US base. Ambiguity with a valid capture is a reject, not a research queue. Record exact evidence and preserve searchable record outside active queue.

## Quality assurance
First review every C for overlooked outdoor/preparedness relevance and every A for unsupported broad inferences. Do not impose an approval quota or manipulate decisions to achieve target counts. Separate fit decision from DM readiness: an A with insufficient unique detail stays qualified but DM pending targeted enrichment, not rejected. Sample-based calibration across batches, monitor reversals and correct systemic errors only with versioned policy changes.

## CRM safeguards
Keep a single workflow_status, no duplicates between active sections. Never overwrite manual approvals/rejections, edited messages, contact history or original capture. Existing importer fills empty fields only; detect conflicts and use audited authorized update route, never silently claim CSV changed nonempty AI recommendations. Do not move a creator to final approved section without Seth's manual approval. Verify actual DB state after writes.
