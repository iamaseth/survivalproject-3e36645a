# Permanent influencer extraction and qualification rules (2026-10-11)

Applies to every Survival Tabs influencer Markdown import, enrichment, and re-review. Do not silently skip steps.

1. Process each creator comprehensively once: parse entire Markdown (YAML frontmatter, links, captions, hashtags, comments if captured, dates, media references), merge all clippings by verified account identity, retain original text and unique evidence, and deduplicate by canonical platform IDs/URLs.
2. Enrich all relevant public linked sources in the same pass: TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, websites, link hubs, Amazon storefronts, business contact pages, agency and public business emails. Verify cross-platform identity; never fabricate contacts, content or engagement.
3. Capture creator niche, relevant actual posts, recency, followers, engagement when available, collaborations, contact route, USA location evidence, provenance URL, checked date, confidence, unknowns, source fingerprint and enrichment version.
4. USA-based creators ONLY qualify for this campaign. A USA audience alone is insufficient. If location is unverified, mark Needs Research, NOT Qualified. Evidence of a non-USA base disqualifies from this campaign. Do not assume nationality from handle or language.
5. Qualification also requires content fit with Survival Tabs. Separate extracted facts, verified facts, inferences, and unknowns.
6. For each qualified creator, CREATE and SAVE an evidence-based, unique personalized outreach DM signed "— Rena, Survival Tabs". Offer a complimentary sample with no posting obligation. Never claim to have watched inaccessible videos. Never auto-send.
7. Preserve all manual approval/rejection decisions, manual DM edits, prior contacts and audit history. Automated results must never override manual decisions. Do not assign to Rena until manually approved; Rena's queue should show approved assigned creators.
8. Persist full research records and original clippings. Skip unchanged already-complete research unless new evidence, missing required verification, or scheduled refresh warrants rechecking. Report file counts, unique creators, duplicates, USA verified/non-USA/unknown, qualified/rejected/research needed, emails and YouTube discovered, DMs created, and failures.
9. UI: compact influencer row with Profile, email shortcut only if email exists, and one-click Copy DM if a draft exists. DM is editable and saveable in expanded details.
